# Plico: what we stand for

The points Plico is built around. Use these for videos, ads, social media, the website and
Reddit posts. Every claim here must stay true of the app as shipped.

## Bhavyam's points (12 Nov 2026, in his words)

- Privacy
- Local storage and has a backup
- No bullshit, only what's needed
- Ever have a thought or find something that you instantly had to save? This answers it: a very
  quick way to write something down that is going on in your mind.
- Different customization options, always listening and always ready to add features that really
  matter, very fast on your own laptop

## The pillars

### 1. Private
Your notes never leave your laptop. No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no ads.
- True today: no account; the only network request is fetching a page title when you paste a link.
- The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check this for themselves.
- Locked folder: notes encrypted with a password (AES-256), recoverable with a security question.

### 2. Yours, stored locally, with a backup
Every note is a plain file in Documents\Plico that opens in any editor. Nothing is locked into the app.
- True today: plain Markdown files, Recently deleted keeps notes for 30 days.
- Before saying "backup" in an ad: version history (on the roadmap) must ship, or say
  "plain files you can back up anywhere" instead.

### 3. Only what's needed
No databases, no plugins, no setup, no AI clutter. Open it and write.
- Ad wording can keep the attitude ("no nonsense, only what you need"); "no bullshit" works for
  Reddit and casual posts, not for store listings.

### 4. Catch a thought the instant it appears
Press Ctrl+Shift+Space in any app, type, press Enter. It folds away and it's saved.
Start a new note or add to one you already have.
- This is the hero of every video: the 5-second demo.
- Measured: opens in about 0.8 seconds from cold, instantly from the tray. App is 5 MB.

### 5. Made the way you like it, and it keeps getting better
Light and dark mode, accent colours, text and highlight colours, note backgrounds.
We read every message and change Plico based on what people actually ask for.
- Back it up with a Send feedback option in the app and a public changelog.
- Word it as what we do ("we read every message"), not "we'll add anything you ask".
